# Overview
Designing Agents using Vogent
Agents are the core building blocks of the Vogent platform. Each agent is a sophisticated AI entity capable of engaging in natural voice conversations. Here’s a guide to all the components and features available for creating and customizing your agents.
## [ConfigurationSet up your agent’s voice, phone numbers, and IVR settings](https://docs.vogent.ai/platform-overview/agents/config/overview)## [ModelDesign your agent’s conversation logic and language understanding](https://docs.vogent.ai/platform-overview/agents/model/overview)## [FunctionsAdd custom functionality and external integrations to your agents](https://docs.vogent.ai/platform-overview/agents/functions)## [ExtractorsConfigure data extraction from conversations for analytics](https://docs.vogent.ai/platform-overview/agents/extractors)## [CounterfactualsTest and validate your agent’s responses in different scenarios](https://docs.vogent.ai/platform-overview/agents/counterfactuals)
